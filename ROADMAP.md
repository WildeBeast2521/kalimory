# Roadmap

Where Kalimory is going. Each block below is a [GitHub milestone](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/milestones);
the issues in it are the plan, and this page is the readable summary. Dates are not promised:
a version ships when its work is done and tested.

Kalimory stays what it is: offline, private and for one person. There is no account, no server,
no tracking and no internet permission, and progressions guide without ever locking anything.
Ideas that need any of those are out of scope.

## 1.1 Trust and polish

Make the app sturdier before it grows.

- Crash reports kept on the phone, shared only if you choose to ([#148](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/148))
- An accessibility pass across every screen ([#149](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/149))
- Screenshot tests, so the look stays consistent ([#150](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/150))
- Native-speaker review of translations through Weblate ([#151](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/151))
- Edit any saved workout fully, including its date ([#156](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/156))
- One time estimate everywhere ([#157](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/157)), whole tab labels on narrow phones ([#194](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/194)) and fresh screenshots ([#158](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/158))
- A careful review of every file for bugs, performance and maintainability before the app grows ([#184](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/184))
- Behind the scenes: dependency updates ([#152](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/152)), dependency verification ([#153](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/153)), a clean lint report ([#154](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/154)) and start-up measurements ([#155](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/155))

## 1.2 Training insight

Answer "am I improving?" from the data the app already has.

- A weekly review ([#159](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/159))
- A body map of volume, recovery and untrained muscles ([#160](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/160))
- A climb chart for each chain you follow ([#161](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/161))
- Personal-best history ([#162](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/162)) and exercise notes ([#163](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/163))
- A year heatmap ([#164](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/164))
- A "what I own" equipment profile ([#165](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/165))
- A larger catalogue, about 500 curated steps, with warm-up, mobility, stretching and conditioning ([#166](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/166))

## 1.3 Progression v2

A progression system that explains itself. Designed first, then built step by step.

- The design ([#167](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/167))
- Warm-up sets and an optional effort rating ([#168](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/168))
- Supersets ([#169](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/issues/169))

## Not planned

Accounts, sync servers, cloud backup, social features, ads, analytics and AI coaching. Exercises
are described with text, form cues and the muscle map rather than photos or videos.

Have an idea or found a bug? Open an issue with one of the templates.
