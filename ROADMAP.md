# Roadmap

Where Kalimory is going. Each block below is a [GitHub milestone](https://github.com/WildeBeast2521/kalimory/milestones),
and each line is one of its issues: ticked when the issue is closed, with the pull request that did it.
Dates are not promised: a version ships when its work is done and tested.

Kalimory stays what it is: offline, private and for one person. There is no account, no server,
no tracking and no internet permission, and progressions guide without ever locking anything.
Ideas that need any of those are out of scope.

## Not planned

Accounts, sync servers, cloud backup, social features, ads, analytics and AI coaching. Exercises
are described with text, form cues and the muscle map rather than photos or videos.

Have an idea or found a bug? Open an issue with one of the templates.
