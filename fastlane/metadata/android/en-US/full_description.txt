Kalimory plans your bodyweight training, runs it with you, and remembers every set. There is no account, and nothing goes online: your workouts stay on your phone.

<b>Today</b>
• This week at a glance, what is due, and a suggestion for what to train next
• Start any workout from one button

<b>Guided workouts</b>
• Rep counting, hold timers and rest timers that keep going with the screen off
• Programs of several exercises, which you can pause and resume later
• Interval routines with timed work and rest
• Log past workouts, with several exercises in one session

<b>157 steps in 29 progression chains</b>
• From the wall push-up to the planche, the dead hang to the front lever, and the assisted squat to the pistol squat
• Every step has form cues, a muscle map, the equipment it needs, and clear standards to start and to move on
• Skills such as the muscle-up and the human flag show the steps they build on
• Nothing is locked: train any step whenever you like

<b>Progressions</b>
• For each chain you follow: your current step, how close you are to moving on, and the dates you started and met each step

<b>Progress</b>
• Calendar, lists, graphs, personal bests and a weekly goal

<b>Your data, your files</b>
• Complete JSON backups, CSV import and export, and recovery files if the database is ever damaged

<b>Calm by design</b>
• Light and dark themes, optional wallpaper colours, and ten languages

<b>Privacy</b>
• No internet permission, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting

Kalimory began as a fork of Calisthenics Memory by Gonbei774 and is free software under the GPL-3.0.
