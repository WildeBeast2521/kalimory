# Privacy

- The app has no internet permission. It cannot send anything anywhere.
- There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
- Your data stays in the app's own storage. It leaves the phone only when you export a backup and move the file yourself.
- Android's own device backup is allowed. If you turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's data in that backup, under your Google account. This is Android's feature, not the app's.

## Permissions

- `FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE` and `WAKE_LOCK` keep workout timers running when the screen is off.
- `FLASHLIGHT` can flash the camera light at the end of a rest.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` shows the running timer in a notification. Android asks for it when your first workout opens; the app works without it.

## When you report a problem

Please do not attach your backups or screenshots of your history to an issue. Describe the problem, or make up a small example.
