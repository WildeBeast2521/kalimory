# Progressions and the catalogue

## The catalogue

Library › Exercise catalogue holds 157 steps in 29 chains. A chain goes from an easier step to a harder one: for example, the Push-up chain starts at the wall push-up and ends at the one-arm push-up.

The chains cover pushing, pulling, dips, rows, squats, lunges, calves, hips and back extensions, core, rotation and side core, grip, explosive work and conditioning; skills such as the handstand, L-sit, muscle-up, front lever, back lever, planche, human flag, back bridge and rings; and lower body, shoulder and wrist mobility.

Each step has:

- a level from 1 to 10, on one scale for every chain;
- a description and form cues;
- the muscles it works, shown on a body map;
- what it needs, such as a pull-up bar;
- a starting standard and a move-on standard, for example "Start at 3 × 8" and "Move on at 3 × 15";
- for skills, "Builds on": steps from other chains that help first.

Nothing is locked. You can add and train any step at any time.

## Progressions

When a step from a chain is in your library, you follow that chain. Progress › Progressions shows, for each chain you follow:

- the step you are working on, and how close your latest session came to its move-on standard;
- "Ready for the next step" once a session met the standard;
- what comes next.

If you set your own target on the exercise, it replaces the step's move-on standard.

To stop following a chain, open it in the catalogue and turn off "Follow this progression".

## Your climb

On a chain's page, each step you have trained shows when you started it, and when you first met its standard. The dates come from your saved workouts.

## Suggestions on Today

Today can suggest what to train, from the chains you follow. It rests a movement for about two days after you train it, and gives a target based on your last session. Suggestions only suggest: nothing starts or changes unless you tap it. Tap "Not today" to hide one, or turn them all off in Settings › Suggestions on Today.
