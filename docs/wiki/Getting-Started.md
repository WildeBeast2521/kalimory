# Getting started

The first time you open the app, a short welcome guide explains the basics. You can skip it, and open it again later from Settings › Welcome guide.

## The four tabs

- **Today.** What you have done this week, what is due, suggestions from your progressions, and a button to start a workout. Tap a day in the week strip to see that day in Progress. Weekly review, beside the week's summary, shows the days you trained, your sets by movement, new bests and the progression steps you mastered, for this week and every week before it.
- **Train.** Start a single exercise, a program or an interval routine, or log a workout you already did.
- **Progress.** Your history as a calendar, a list and graphs, plus your progressions and exercise targets. The figure icon at the top opens the body map, with three views: **Sets** shades each muscle by the sets it got a week over 1 week, 1 month or 3 months, counting a set in full for the muscles a step mainly works and as half for those that help; **Recent** shows the muscles you worked in the last few days, and which are recovering from sets you rated with 1 rep left or none; **Not worked** lists the muscles that got no sets in the period. Mobility, exercises not linked to the catalogue and interval work are listed apart, never dropped.
- **Library.** Your exercises and groups, programs, interval routines, the To Do list, the exercise catalogue, and Settings.

## Your first workout

1. Add an exercise. Either create your own in Library › Exercises and groups, or open Library › Exercise catalogue, pick a chain and a step, and tap "Add to my exercises".
2. Go to Train and start it from Quick start.
3. Do your sets. The app counts, times holds and rests, and ticks each set off.
4. When you finish, the summary shows your totals and any new personal best.

## Search

Each list with a search field finds any name it shows. In Library › Exercises and groups, a group's name shows the whole group. In the lists where you pick an exercise, an exercise is found by its own name or its group's, and each result shows its group. Programs and interval routines are also found by the exercises they hold, and a row found that way says which, such as "Includes Dips". Case and accents don't matter, nor do Arabic short vowels, and the words can come in any order. The exercise catalogue also searches muscles and equipment.

## A weekly goal

Settings › Weekly goal sets how many days a week you want to train, from 2 to 6, or Off. Today then shows "This week: 2 of 4" instead of "2 of 7".

## Look and language

Settings › Theme offers Follow system, Light, Dark and AMOLED (true black, for OLED screens). On Android 12 and later, Dynamic colours can take the colours of your wallpaper instead of the app's own. Settings › Language lists every language the app ships in, each in its own name.
